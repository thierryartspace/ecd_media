Privacy policy

What we collect

Ndaah Academy collects information you provide when you work with the school or when your child attends the school. This includes identity details, contact information, academic records, attendance, assessment and finance information needed to run the school.

How we use your data

We use your data in different ways to support teaching, learning, school administration, communication with parents and guardians, and to meet our legal obligations. Data is processed only for legitimate school purposes.

Sharing your data

We may share your data with legal authorities or with our national school authorities — including NESA, MINEDUC and HEC — when they request it for verification, reporting or compliance.

Keeping your data safe

Your data is safe with us. We apply strict access controls, audit trails and secure storage to make sure your information is only seen by authorised staff.

Your right to your data

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you at any time. Use the Download my data option in Settings or contact the school administration.

Last updated: 12 January 2024
Terms of use

Account activity

When you log in or while your account is active, we record what you do on the platform — such as the actions you take, the records you view or modify, and the time of each action.

Identity assumption

We believe that actions performed through your account are done by you. Keep your password private and contact the administration immediately if you suspect someone else has access to your account.

Acceptable use

  • Use the platform only for school-related purposes.
  • Do not share your account with anyone else.
  • Do not attempt to access data you are not authorised to see.
  • Report any suspicious activity to the school administration.

Your data rights

You can request your data at any time. We will provide a copy of the activity and information linked to your account.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of the platform means you accept the latest version.

Last updated: 12 January 2024
